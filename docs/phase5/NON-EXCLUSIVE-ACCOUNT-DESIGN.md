# Non-exclusive subaccount policy — development design for review

**Status:** Proposal only, 2026-09-25. Not implemented or approved. No safety contract is changed by this document. The existing exclusive-control path remains intact; its real `exclusiveControlVerified` value remains `false`. Keep environment and database execution flags OFF, kill switch ON, and the current no-live-order barrier in place. Do not create or fund a subaccount, publish, send an exchange order, or cancel one for this review.

## 1. Claim and boundary

The proposed policy is **observed, non-exclusive exposure**, not "exclusive control with a weaker proof." It may be considered only after a separate review deliberately accepts that another authorized actor can trade or transfer at any time. It must have its own named policy, evidence record, and approval path; it must **never** set `exclusiveControlVerified` to `true`, reuse a mocked proof in real execution, or silently fall back from the exclusive mode. The existing default and all current runtime gates stay unchanged until that separate decision.

An eligible future implementation would be limited to **one immutable bot entry attempt and at most one identified exit attempt in one run**. It may conservatively reject the account at any point. "Clean" means *no outside activity observed in the available complete evidence at the time of the read*, not proof that another actor cannot act in the next millisecond. An external lease in this app or its database serializes only this app, never Kalshi's owner, other keys, or sub-users.

Kalshi says the owner and each sub-user can act on **all subaccounts within their permissions**; sub-user permissions are account-wide, and orders/fills do not record the sub-user. Numbered subaccounts are currently API-only, so do not assert that the web/mobile UI itself can select one. A scoped bot key restricts **that key**, not every other possible actor. None of the cited documentation provides an exchange-enforced exclusive lease for a subaccount.

No credential is requested by this design. The current development environment has no selected numbered subaccount or verifier credentials. Configuring or checking actual account access belongs to a separately authorized setup step; do not turn on trading to perform it.

## 3. Proposed lifecycle (not implemented)

1. **Disabled / baseline review.** All real execution gates remain OFF. In isolated development tests only, build a mock account snapshot and complete current/history/transfer scans. Reject an unexplained opening position, open order, unknown fill, old same-market activity, missing history, inconsistent cash, or unknown key scope. Capture a versioned baseline of order/fill IDs and amounts, position, cash, transfer cursor/history, subaccount, policy ID, and fee evidence. The documented transfer listing alone is not proof of a complete cash ledger: verify coverage of all relevant balance-changing events separately, or block eligibility. Transfers used to fund the account would have to predate this baseline; this document authorizes none.
2. **One bot reservation.** In the proposed mode only, after future approval, lock the run/config and immutable ledger rows, reserve a unique client order ID and fee-bound budget once, and recheck the current evidence immediately before the *one* submission. Recheck linked orders, account-wide order/fill changes, all positions, open orders, balance, and transfer history. Any unexplained or incomplete result blocks. The lock prevents *our own* duplicate submissions, not an outside order after the last GET.
3. **Observed execution.** A matching exchange ID/client ID/status and authoritative fill totals may be attributed to this bot reservation; a client ID alone is not a cryptographic proof of actor identity. Poll/read complete evidence while the run is unresolved. Foreign, duplicate, changed, or ambiguous evidence immediately stops **new mutations**, persists a reason and last complete checkpoint, and moves to frozen manual review. Do not mark a foreign cash flow as bot P&L.
4. **Exit and cancellation.** Only a separately proven filled bot position may qualify for a future reviewed exit policy. Its proposed constraints are quantity limits, fresh fee/quote evidence, exact identity checks, and reduce-only IOC; these are not implemented for non-exclusive mode. If inventory has changed, a different actor has appeared, or the position cannot be attributed, **do not issue a blind sell or an automatic replacement**. Freeze and refer the position for supervised action; this may leave bot inventory exposed. **No bot DELETE is authorized in the proposed mode.** The existing frozen cancellation routine calls the exclusive-control proof and cannot be reused as-is. An identified resting bot exit would need a separately reviewed, explicitly authorized manual intervention procedure, with GET-only follow-up; until that exists, the non-exclusive mode cannot be considered implementation-ready for real execution.
5. **Restart.** Start with process-local readiness false. With flags OFF, perform GET-only reconciliation of all known orders, fills, status, positions, settlements, balance, and transfers. A pending/unknown POST, outstanding exit, cancellation claim, foreign activity, transfer, incomplete history, or changed checkpoint stays frozen. Verified late fills may update the ledger without restoring order authority. The current exclusive startup code can branch to cancellation for a frozen outstanding exit; a future non-exclusive dispatcher **must route before that branch and prohibit startup POST and DELETE**, without altering the exclusive branch. This is an implementation prerequisite, not current behavior guaranteed by this document. No manual flip of the two database flags may reopen this run; any future run requires a separately reviewed baseline and explicit decision. No automatic restart after an exit.

### Incident rules

| Evidence | Required proposed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Outside trade** on any market in the selected subaccount, including an order later canceled or net-zero offsetting fills | Freeze all bot mutations and retain full order/fill evidence. Never net it away or relabel it as a bot fill. A foreign order between GET and POST can still evade prevention; monitoring can only detect it later. |
| **Cash transfer**, incoming or outgoing, before/after an account read | Freeze; capture paginated transfer IDs, amount, source/destination, time, and cash checkpoints. Reconcile it as a cash flow, **not P&L**. Even an expected transfer during an active run forces manual review/re-baselining. The documented transfer endpoint must not be assumed to cover every account cash movement, and multiple GETs are not atomic. A balance delta without independently adequate cash-flow evidence is unresolved. |
| **Conflicting, duplicate, or missing position**; signed/side mismatch; reduced inventory | Freeze; no fresh buy, replacement exit, or sell based solely on local FIFO. Require account-wide order/fill/settlement evidence and human resolution. Do not assume a reduce-only order guarantees desired ownership or completion. |
| **Unknown entry POST**, no exchange ID, timeout, 5xx, or inconsistent success body | Keep the immutable reserved attempt, stop further POSTs, query signed current+historical evidence for an *exact* identity; absence from a single GET does not prove rejection. Identity linkage is administrative/frozen only. Never retry the entry or spend its reservation again. |
| **Unknown exit POST or DELETE**, partial fill, canceled/no-fill IOC | Freeze, preserve local identity and any existing single-use cancellation claim, read authenticated status/fills repeatedly as needed, and never automatically replace. In proposed non-exclusive mode **no bot cancellation is available**, even for an identified resting exit. Require separate supervised intervention and read-only verification; do not route through the exclusive-mode cancellation path. |
| **Incomplete reads, stale scan, missing archived page, authorization error, transfer endpoint denied, or DB ledger change mid-scan** | No eligible state, no assumed zero records, no budget recycling. Keep all gates OFF; report the reason for manual review. |

### What the $1 number means

The existing `LIVE_TEST_CAP_MICROS = 1_000_000` is an **admission/reservation bound for modeled bot cash exposure on its single order**: nominal requested stake plus fee/rounding entry top-up and the worst verified additional negative net exit cash debit, including the conservative possible fill fragmentation. It is checked before reservation and under the DB lock. It is **not** a promise that the exchange account cannot lose more than $1, not a cap on another actor's losses, and not an enforceable stop-loss on an open position. The planned $25 bankroll/configuration and `SESSION_CAP_MICROS` constant are likewise **not an account-wide loss guarantee**; the current one-entry rule, not a reusable $25 budget, is the operative run constraint.

An outside trader can open or enlarge exposure, consume cash, incur fees, transfer funds, offset net position, or change settlement attribution. Even the bot's exit could become impossible or expensive after its last check. Consequently account equity or realized P&L could decline by **more than $1**, and funds in the subaccount could be lost independently of the modeled bot order. Bounded bot submission is a narrower statement than bounded total account loss.

**Unenforceable against another actor:** no local lock can prevent a trade or transfer between the last GET and the bot POST; repeated or net-zero outside activity may evade balance/position-only comparisons; an outside actor can change inventory after an exit check or drain funds needed for an exit; and a transfer listing does not prove the absence of other balance-changing mechanisms or an atomic cash snapshot. This policy can detect evidence and stop *our* later mutations, not prevent the outside actor's action or guarantee a maximum account loss.

## 4. Acceptance tests required *before* changing the contract

All tests below are **future isolated-development tests**: disposable guarded development schema, injected/mock exchange GET/POST/DELETE/transfer responses, zero real exchange mutations, and assertions that real flags/kill switch remain disabled. They are review criteria, not a claim that they pass today. Keep the current exclusive-mode tests intact and passing.

| Test family / cases to implement | Acceptance result |
| --- | --- |
| **Modes and migration:** absent/unknown proposed policy, mock account without a valid baseline, attempted selector 0 or 64, and manual DB flag flip in either mode; repeat all existing exclusive-control and authorization tests. | Default exclusive mode behaves exactly as before and never fabricates `exclusiveControlVerified`; proposed mode cannot activate via a flag flip, test-only fixture, or fallback. No production route to trading exists merely because a design is documented. |
| **Minimum permissions:** matching numbered key with `read`+`write::trade`; wrong subaccount, read-only bot key, broad/unrestricted key, missing/denied key listing, missing auditor read permission for transfers. | Only the exact scoped candidate passes *mock policy metadata*; all others remain disabled. Auditor failure is a block, not an empty transfer list. No test sends a real key or order. |
| **Budget boundaries:** modeled total exactly $1 and $1 plus one micro-dollar; worst fee fragmentation, non-direct cent rounding, verified zero and negative net exit cash; unknown/change in terms; one rejected/partial/unknown prior attempt. | Exactly $1 is eligible only with complete evidence; one micro over and every unknown fee block. A prior attempt is never recycled. Test explicitly shows an outside actor's >$1 loss does **not** become falsely reported as satisfying an account-wide cap. |
| **Outside orders/fills:** foreign resting order, canceled zero-fill order, aged-out fill, same-ticker offsetting buy/sell, different-market order, duplicated/spoofed client ID, order/status count conflict, foreign activity between two scans. | Any observed unexplained order/fill blocks all mutations; net-zero positions are not treated as clean. No new bot POST or unverified DELETE; evidence and reason survive restart. |
| **GET→POST race:** inject outside activity after the final preflight GET but before the mock POST, then also just after a mock POST. | The first can still produce a bot order in a non-exclusive account: test must **document the unpreventable race**, never assert a false guarantee. On the next observable read it freezes, attributes neither outside fill to the bot nor account loss to the $1 budget, and makes no second POST. If reviewers require prevention rather than detection, reject this design. |
| **Transfers and cash:** pre-baseline funding; incoming/outgoing active-run transfer; zero-net pair; cursor loop, missing page, duplicate ID, mismatch in amount, unrelated balance jump, auditor 403; transfer during pagination; transfer after the final GET and before mock POST; other balance-changing event not in the transfer feed. | Only independently explained pre-baseline cash is accepted. Any observed active-run transfer or ambiguous balance is frozen; transfer is excluded from P&L. Missing/denied history and an unverified ledger scope block eligibility. Transfer after the last GET **can race through**, so the test asserts detection/freeze on the next complete read, not prevention or a loss cap. |
| **Positions and exit:** full/partial bot fill, foreign inventory shrink/increase/opposite side, duplicate ticker, account/local fill mismatch, reduce-only rejection or cap, resting/terminal IOC, no-fill and late partial fill. | Exit quantity never exceeds verified bot-owned inventory. On conflict there is no automatic sell, replacement, or second entry. Verified late fills can be booked GET-only while readiness remains false. |
| **Uncertain orders/cancellation:** timeout after accepted entry/exit POST; 400 definite rejection; invalid response; historical 404; attempted route into the existing frozen cancel procedure; inherited cancellation claim and delayed fill; repeated callers. | One durable reservation, at most one mock POST per allowed intent and **zero bot DELETEs in proposed mode**; no retry on unknown outcomes. Exact account/order/fill proof is needed before attribution. Ambiguity stays frozen across replay. Existing exclusive-mode one-claim cancellation tests must still pass independently. |
| **Crashes/restarts:** crash before and after POST, before exchange ID persistence, during ledger transaction, after cancellation claim, after terminal unclaimed IOC, an identified resting exit with flags OFF, plus manual flag reenable. | At most one bot attempt; frozen reads may reconcile verified changes exactly once. Future proposed-mode dispatch must bypass the current exclusive startup cancellation branch: **no startup POST/DELETE** and no automatic readiness after any exit, outside activity, or transfer. The exclusive path's behavior remains unchanged. |

## 5. Explicit review decision

Before anyone implements this proposed mode, reviewers must choose whether **detection plus a limited bot-order admission budget**, with an unavoidable external-actor race and no account-wide loss guarantee, is acceptable. If it is not, the only approved course is to retain paper/shadow operation under the existing exclusive-control gate unless Kalshi provides an independently verifiable exchange-enforced sole-actor mechanism. Passing isolated tests alone would **not** authorize publishing, enabling flags, funding, or real-order validation; each would require separate approval and real-account evidence.
